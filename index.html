Review design guardrails

This workspace is governed by a calm, intentional design system. Colors, typography, spacing, and motion are tightly constrained to avoid visual drift and noise.

  • Off‑white background, near‑black text, deep red accent only.
  • Serif headings, clean sans‑serif body, limited type sizes.
  • Spacing system of 8, 16, 24, 40, 64px only.
  • No gradients, glassmorphism, neon, or playful animation.
